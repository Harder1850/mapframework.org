3. `Relational_Intelligence_Master_Framework_and_Execution_Plan-1.docx` — Relational Intelligence foundation, context/abstraction/transfer, public platform strategy, product/experiment family, disclosure strategy, and historical conceptual baseline.
4. Web on Demand prototype — dark scientific visual system, sticky navigation, network/constellation interaction, concept-library search, page architecture, and responsive patterns.
5. ChatGPT Sites prototype/reference — MAP/RI/RCP/ALIVE layering, richer Delta protocol framing, diagnostic and publishing-strategy cues.
6. Approved visual assets: `31.png`–`35.png`, preserved under `public/assets/source/` and web-optimized under `public/assets/visuals/`.

Where these sources conflict, `docs/CONFLICT_RECONCILIATION.md` records the production decision.
